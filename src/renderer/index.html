Cyprus VAT · invoices to fetch

Stripe can only search invoices by the date they were created, but the VAT report goes by the invoice date (when it was finalized, or the date set on it). By default only invoices created within the VAT period are fetched, which is fastest. A wider search also finds invoices finalized late or back-dated into the period, but fetches more invoices and is slower.

Invoice statuses

Paid, open and uncollectible are the finalized invoices that count for VAT. Draft and void invoices are not VAT invoices: include them only to look for mistakes. They are flagged, but counted in the totals.
